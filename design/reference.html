App icons (squircle preview of the full-bleed exports)

Rules

  • Orbit ring r 8.5 at 12,12, stroke 2.5, round ends
  • Paper plane on the orbit at -45°, flying clockwise
  • Ring ends stop exactly 1.5 units from the plane
  • Mark spans 60% of the app icon, 40% of the Android adaptive canvas
  • Neutral icon tiles only, never a colored tile
  • In-app mark is 26px, slightly larger than 22px nav icons
