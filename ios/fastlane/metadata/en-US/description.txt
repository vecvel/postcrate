Ever finished a post and then wondered where you actually shared it? Postcrate keeps every post in one place and remembers where each one went.

Write once, share anywhere
• Write a post once, with its photos, videos and files
• Send it to Instagram, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, YouTube or any app on your phone through the system share sheet
• See per-app character counts while you write

Remember where it went
• Every share is recorded, with the app and the time
• Save the link to the live post and open it later

Know your video fits
• Duration, size and format checks for the apps you are posting to, including Reels and Shorts

Private by design
• No account, no server, no tracking
• Everything stays on your phone
• Backups are a single file you keep wherever you like

Free and open source: github.com/vecvel/postcrate
